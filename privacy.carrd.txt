Privacy Policy

URSACORP LLC. Applies to our Atlassian Marketplace apps: Issue Aging Chart,
Worklog Pivot, Sprint Countdown, and Epic Lens.

Effective date: 2026-10-28


How the apps work

The apps use Atlassian Forge to host their interfaces inside Jira. The
interface runs in your browser and reads Jira data, such as issues, boards,
sprints and worklogs, through Atlassian's APIs as the signed-in viewer.
Jira checks that viewer's access permissions.

Report calculations, grouping, filtering and CSV creation take place in the
browser. Browser-based versions call Jira using the Forge bridge. Earlier
versions of Worklog Pivot and Epic Lens also process Jira API responses in
Atlassian-hosted Forge resolver functions. Both paths use the viewer's Jira
permissions. The apps do not send Jira report content to a URSACORP server,
use Forge Storage, or maintain an app database of that content.

The app manifests contain no external egress permissions or remote services.
We add no external analytics, tracking pixels or telemetry to the apps. We do
not sell Jira data. This does not prevent you from downloading or sharing an
export you have chosen to create.


What stays on your device

Jira responses and calculated reports are held in the app page's browser
memory. They are not saved by us as a separate copy of your Jira records.
Jira's own records remain subject to your site's retention and access rules.

Exports you trigger, such as Worklog Pivot CSV files, are generated in your
browser and downloaded to your device. You decide where to save or share them
and when to delete them. Small display preferences, such as collapsed groups
in Epic Lens, may remain in browser local storage until you clear that storage.
Issue Aging Chart and Sprint Countdown save the settings you choose in Jira's
dashboard gadget configuration, not in a URSACORP database or Forge Storage.


Installation, operational and support information

Atlassian makes installation details and operational usage metadata available
to app vendors. This can include site or installation identifiers, installation
status, dates, and platform usage or error counts. We use this information to
operate and support the apps, understand adoption and review hosting usage.
These platform records do not provide the content of your reports, confirm a
completed report or CSV export, or measure every browser interaction. Browser
requests do not generate app-resolver invocation metrics.

We may retain operational records for those purposes. When sharing findings
publicly, we use aggregate or de-identified summaries rather than customer site
identifiers. We do not add a separate tracking service to replace platform
metrics.

If you contact us, we receive the email address and information you choose to
send. Please do not include confidential Jira content, customer information or
credentials in support messages. We use the correspondence to answer your
request and maintain support records. Contact us about information you have
sent or a deletion request at support@ursacorp.org.


Your Jira permissions

Every Jira API request runs as the signed-in viewer. None of the apps requests
write permission. The apps use these read-only scopes:

- Issue Aging Chart: read:jira-work
- Worklog Pivot: read:jira-work
- Sprint Countdown: read:board-scope:jira-software, read:sprint:jira-software,
  read:issue-details:jira, read:jql:jira, read:project:jira
- Epic Lens: read:board-scope:jira-software, read:sprint:jira-software,
  read:issue-details:jira, read:jql:jira, read:project:jira,
  read:audit-log:jira, read:avatar:jira, read:field-configuration:jira,
  read:issue-meta:jira

Sprint Countdown and Epic Lens use granular scopes for Jira Software's agile
APIs. Epic Lens's audit-log, avatar, field-configuration and issue-meta scopes
are included for the enhanced issue-search endpoint. The app does not use them
to read the audit log or perform a separate audit-log analysis.

The documentation for each app explains its scopes. Moving Worklog Pivot and
Epic Lens requests from Forge resolvers to the browser does not add scopes.


Security and contact

The apps never ask for your Atlassian password, personal access token or other
shared secret, and hold no customer credentials. Our security policy describes
how we handle vulnerabilities and incidents. Browser execution and the absence
of an app database do not make an application immune to security defects.

Security reports: security@ursacorp.org
Support and privacy questions: support@ursacorp.org
Security policy: https://docs.ursacorp.org/security/

If app behavior changes in a way that affects this policy, we will update this
page and its effective date before shipping that change.
