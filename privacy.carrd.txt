Privacy Policy

URSACORP LLC — applies to our Atlassian Marketplace apps: Issue Aging Chart,
Worklog Pivot, Sprint Countdown, and Epic Lens.

Effective date: 2026-08-19.


The short version

These apps do not collect, store, transmit, or sell any of your data. There is
nothing to opt out of, because nothing is gathered.


How the apps work

All four apps are built on Atlassian Forge and run entirely on Atlassian's
infrastructure, inside your own Jira site. They read Jira data (issues, sprints,
worklogs) through Atlassian's APIs using the permissions of the person viewing
the page, render it in your browser, and discard it. They are eligible for
Atlassian's Runs on Atlassian program, which means they make no network calls
outside Atlassian at all.


What we do not do

- No servers of our own — no data ever leaves Atlassian's infrastructure.
- No analytics, tracking pixels, or telemetry of any kind.
- No accounts, cookies, or identifiers created by us.
- No data shared with, or sold to, anyone.


Data retention

Exports you trigger yourself (for example a CSV download in Worklog Pivot) are
generated in your browser and saved only to your device. Small per-user display
preferences (for example which groups you collapsed) may be kept in your own
browser's local storage; they never leave your machine.


Your Jira permissions are the boundary

The apps can only show you what you could already see in Jira yourself. Every
Jira request runs as the signed-in viewer. None of the apps requests any write
permission.

Each app requests the smallest read-only permission set that its Jira API calls
actually require:

- Issue Aging Chart: read:jira-work
- Worklog Pivot: read:jira-work
- Sprint Countdown: read:board-scope:jira-software, read:sprint:jira-software,
  read:issue-details:jira, read:jql:jira, read:project:jira
- Epic Lens: read:board-scope:jira-software, read:sprint:jira-software,
  read:issue-details:jira, read:jql:jira, read:project:jira,
  read:audit-log:jira, read:avatar:jira, read:field-configuration:jira,
  read:issue-meta:jira

Sprint Countdown and Epic Lens use granular scopes because Jira's agile
endpoints reject the older read:jira-work scope. The four extra scopes on Epic
Lens (audit-log, avatar, field-configuration, issue-meta) are the set Atlassian
documents as required for the /rest/api/3/search/jql endpoint. Epic Lens does
not read your audit log, and no app uses any of these scopes for anything
beyond rendering the issue list you see on screen.

The exact permission list for each app is also shown on its Atlassian
Marketplace listing page.


Security

Report a security issue to ursacorp.llc@gmail.com.

The apps hold no credentials of yours. They never ask for an Atlassian Personal
Access Token, an account password, or any other shared secret. They store no
data, so there is no store to breach. Because they run on Atlassian Forge with
no outbound network access, a compromise of an app cannot send your Jira data
to a third party.


Changes and contact

If an app's behavior ever changes in a way that affects this policy, we will
update this page and the effective date before shipping the change.
