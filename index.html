URSACORP Apps

Small, fast, honest tools for Jira. Built on Atlassian Forge; no data ever leaves your Atlassian site.

  • Issue Aging Chart — see which in-progress work is silently getting old, judged against your own cycle times.
  • Epic Lens — a fast, keyboard-driven sprint view grouped by epic, for people who liked GitHub Projects.
  • Sprint Countdown — a big, glanceable countdown to the end of your active sprint.
  • Worklog Pivot — who logged how much time on what, this month, with one-click CSV.
